# Key Limitations, Validity, and Nonclaims

1. **Survivorship bias is severe:** the vendor includes only S&P 500 constituents *as of Nov 2026*. Former constituents and delisted securities are excluded from the full lookback. A current-member universe examined retrospectively is not a historically investable S&P 500 selection mechanism.
2. **No financial statement features:** inputs contain adjusted prices, OHLCV, published portfolio-factor and industry returns, not point-in-time earnings, book value, analyst revisions, announcement times, profitability, or asset growth. Do not describe this repository as a tested value/quality/growth alpha system.
3. **No real execution data:** range and volatility proxies cannot reveal executable bid–ask spread or actual market impact. The numerical optimizer is not an order router, risk platform, OMS, or real-time execution system.
4. **Entry prices are modeled next-open prices:** a vendor-derived adjusted open is computed, not observed in a separate total-return opening auction feed. Latency, opening imbalance, partial fills, and bid/ask are ignored.
5. **Factors are revised-vintage research files:** the Kenneth French CSV archives are dated Sept 2026, not a vintage-accurate history of factor data as known in 2020. This matters for strict historical replication.
6. **Tests are software checks:** 23 unit/integration checks validate selected invariants and empirical outputs. They do not prove predictive ability, financial causality, robustness to market regimes, absence of model specification error, or compliance with industry reporting standards.
7. **Concentration and benchmark:** top 40 liquid current-member large caps are **not** the global stock universe, market-cap-weighted S&P 500 or Russell 1000. The equal-weight dynamic same-basket benchmark is a comparison portfolio, not a passive exchange-traded index.
8. **Return-forecast hypothesis not supported:** the recorded rank IC is negative and not significant. Cost-aware active performance is also not significant. Favor rigorous negative findings over manufactured success.
9. **No hindsight parameter selection is claimed:** risk aversion, signal windows, Ridge penalty, impact coefficient, position limit and factor caps were selected as interpretable research assumptions, but they were not jointly cross-validated ex ante and may be sensitive to design choices.
10. **Global and proprietary risks absent:** no emerging markets, country-level alpha, rates, FX, oil risk, broker constraints, or Acadian proprietary alpha-risk isolation is reconstructed.

**Next steps before any investment-performance claim:** obtain a point-in-time security master with delistings and total-return histories; point-in-time SEC filing fundamentals with release timestamps; actual bid/ask or TAQ-style quotes and trade price prints; historical index weights and sectors; nested walk-forward parameter validation; realistic delayed execution and market-capacity sensitivity; a longer and truly out-of-sample evaluation with a pre-registered hypothesis.
