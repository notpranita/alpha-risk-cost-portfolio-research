The included historical daily OHLCV file was downloaded by the user from Aethereal Data and is described by the provider as **free for research and education**. This project is intended for those purposes, not resale or commercial distribution. Aethereal's license wording may change; consult https://dataset.aethereal.top/datasets/sp500-stock-prices for current reuse terms. Citation: Aethereal (2026), *S&P 500 Stock Prices*, accessed Nov 12, 2026.

The 49 industry portfolios, Fama/French 5 factors and Momentum Factor datasets originate from the Kenneth R. French Data Library at Dartmouth / Tuck. Original archives are preserved; the file content states it was produced with the September 2026 CRSP database. Cite: https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/data_library.html.

The Acadian Investor Forum is **referenced, not reproduced** within the source files: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1748824/000162828026036160/a2026-masterfilexirforum.htm. The public presentation marks its investment-process diagrams as stylized; the original intellectual property belongs to Acadian Asset Management.
